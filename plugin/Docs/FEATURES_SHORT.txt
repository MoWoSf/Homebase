HOMEBASE FOR X-PLANE 12  (version 1.0)
Your FBO, your hangar, your ground crew.

One global plugin for every aircraft you fly. Start in an FBO lounge or in your own private hangar,
walk round your aircraft, and let the ground services come to you.

KEY FEATURES

- Works with any aircraft: Homebase measures it from its 3D models for walking round it, the
  hangar fit check and vehicles that keep clear of wings, engines and tail.
- Data packs included for the Aerobask Falcon 8X and the AKD Studio G650 / G650ER: airstair,
  cabin, seats, luggage hold and refuelling point.
- FBO lounge with reception, VIP bar, crew briefing room and real lights at night. The screens
  show the departure board (VATSIM), weather, ATIS and your SimBrief OFP. A car drives you to
  your aircraft.
- Private hangars in four sizes (L, M, S, XS). Place them at any airport with the tablet's map.
  Each has its own door and lights, and you start upstairs at the bar.
- Ground services ordered from the phone:
  - a luggage van that loads your bags into the hold;
  - a passenger car;
  - turnarounds for your next SimBrief flight.
- Fuel truck with an attendant who handles the hose and nozzle. Order an amount, your SimBrief
  ramp fuel, or full tanks. Fuelling time: Realistic, Fast or Instant.
- Tow tug with a remote. Push back and tow; HOME parks the aircraft back in its hangar.
- Tablet with your airframes: registration, livery and homebase. Each airframe keeps its fuel,
  parking spot and flight hours. A world map shows where they all are.
- First-person walkaround through the cabin, airstair, apron, lounge and hangar, with footsteps
  on every surface. Works with X-Camera.
- Your own aircraft: Plugins > Homebase > AI profile kit writes a ready-to-use AI prompt with
  the aircraft's measurements, to make a data pack for it.
- Automatic updates through the free SkunkCrafts Updater.

INSTALLATION
Unpack Homebase.zip into your X-Plane 12 folder (the one with the X-Plane program). Then start X-Plane.

REQUIREMENTS
X-Plane 12 on Windows, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) or Linux, 64-bit.
macOS: after unpacking, run in the Terminal once:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/X-Plane 12/Resources/plugins/Homebase"
Optional: a SimBrief ID, a VATSIM CID, the SkunkCrafts Updater.

Please report problems on our Discord and attach your Log.txt.
