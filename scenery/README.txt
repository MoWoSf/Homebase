Private Hangar Homebases
========================

The private hangar of the FA8X Hangar and GLF650 Hangar plugins. It has no place of its own:
the plugins' tablet places it at your homebases (Tablet -> Homebase). Each hangar you place
is a file in hangars/, and the plugin writes the scenery tiles (Earth nav data) from them.

X-Plane reads scenery when it starts: restart X-Plane after placing or moving a hangar.
Leave this folder's name as it is; the plugins look for it.

Textures: Poly Haven (CC0). Library objects: X-Plane's own.
